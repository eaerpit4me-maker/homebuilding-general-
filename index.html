How to use this guide

Start with the work. Add JDE when the process makes sense.

01Understand the stage

Learn the outcome, sequence, decisions and common failure points in plain language.

04Check your understanding

Answer a short scenario question before moving to the next stage.

Important: real homebuilding is not perfectly linear. Sales, permitting, design, procurement and construction planning often overlap. The stages give learners a reliable mental model while the handoffs show where coordination is required.
